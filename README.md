# 🌌 Kerbopause-Mod 🌌
An apocalyptic Alternate Universe (AU) expansion for Kerbal Space Program.

## 📖 The Lore & Scale
Something terrifying is approaching. An intergalactic world-killer—**Nibirum**—has violently ripped into our star system, tearing a physical breach through our protective perimeter boundaries. 

In this Alternate Universe, the star system is wrapped in realistic, colossal cosmic safeguards designed to shield the inner planets from deep-space threats:
*   🔴 **The Kerbopause:** A massive, glowing 3D red sphere acting as the magnetic friction wall where solar winds crash into the interstellar medium.
*   ⚪ **The Kerman Cloud:** A gargantuan, frosty-white outer sphere spanning a massive **1 Kerbal Light Year (1 KLY)** thick, protecting the system from neighboring stars.

While charting the edge of the world following an aggressive 10-G atmospheric slingshot around Jool, a lone deep-space probe accidentally stumbled upon a hidden neighborhood.

## 🪐 Planetary Manifest

### ☄️ Sedurim (The Accidental Dwarf)
*   **Type:** Ultra-Cold Dwarf Planet / Sedna Analog
*   **Radius:** ~100 km (Compact geometry with massive, jagged mountain ridges)
*   **Orbit:** Highly inclined (11.93°) and extremely eccentric (0.857) 1,200-year oval loop that plunges deep into the dark heart of the Kerman Cloud.
*   **Surface:** A weather-beaten, dark copper-rose crust covered in ancient tholins and volcanic fissures.

### 🛑 Nibirum (The Fiery Intruder)
*   **Type:** Rogue Gas Giant / Failed Star Brown Dwarf Analog
*   **Radius:** 6,000 km (A colossal gas giant matching Jool's scale with a high 1.7g surface gravity)
*   **Orbit:** Tilted on a hyper-chaotic doomsday path (23.45° inclination) with an extreme 0.985 eccentricity—essentially falling in a straight line from the edge of the system.
*   **Atmosphere:** A deep, crushing blood-red gas envelope layered with turbulent, glowing crimson and hot-pink thermal bands.

## 🛠️ Tester Instructions (For Kiwimaster6355)
Hello! To test these custom planets and spheres in-game, please follow these file management guidelines:

### 📄 Repository File Checklist
Ensure all your files are sitting together in a single flat directory list:
*   `Sedurim.cfg` (Planet parameters)
*   `Nibirum.cfg` (Rogue giant parameters)
*   `SpaceSpheres.cfg` (Procedural 3D Atmosphere codes for the boundaries)
*   `SedurimClr.png` (Color texture map)
*   `SedurimHeight.png` (Terrain elevation map)
*   `SedurimNrm.png` (Lighting normal map)
*   `Nibirum.jpeg` (Brown dwarf gas texture map)

### 🚀 Setup Steps
1. Make sure you have **Kopernicus** and its required dependencies installed in your KSP directory.
2. Download all the files from this repository.
3. Place them all directly inside a single folder within your game's `GameData` directory.
4. **CRUCIAL:** Your folder *must* be named exactly **`Kerbopause-Mod`** so the configuration links can find your texture files!
5. Launch KSP. The engine will automatically compile your `.bin` cache files on the first load.
6. Open the Tracking Station to check Nibirum's terrifying drop trajectory, inspect Sedurim's rugged cliffs, and fly straight through the giant 3D spheres!
